How to play

  1. 1

    Tap a suspect. Hear their clue.

    Every clue is one short fact in plain words. Agnes starts with “Oh dear!”, Brock says “Bro!”, Biscuit sniffs, Marcel mimes. Every clue is true.

  2. 2

    Cross out the innocent.

    Mark someone innocent and they sigh “Phew!”. The game tells you when the clues really prove it, and says “Prove it!” when they don’t yet.

  3. 3

    Make ONE guess.

    No take-backs. Get it right and it’s GOTCHA! Get it wrong and the game shows you, step by step, how to solve it.
